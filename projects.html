Engineering Lifecycle: From Problem to Decision Support

A disciplined 4-stage pipeline applied across every case study to ensure methodological integrity and actionable value.

Stage 01 // Definition

IFRS & Statutory Scoping

Formulating analytical requirements against regulatory frameworks (IAS 1, IFRS 15, IFRS 9). Ensuring compliance with statutory financial reporting conventions and establishing clear objective metrics.

Stage 02 // Data Ingestion

Normalization & Integrity Controls

Ingesting raw general ledgers or public financial statements. Applying automated Python scripts or Excel Power Query to eliminate discrepancies and enforce double-entry validation.

Stage 03 // Computation

Dynamic Formulaic Modeling

Building sensitivity models with dynamic arrays (XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH). Executing cross-sectional ratio decompositions, cash flow projections, and liquidity stress testing.

Stage 04 // Delivery

Executive Board-Ready Delivery

Synthesizing complex multi-year figures into visual dashboards with clear conditional formatting, variance alerts, and concise executive summary commentary for leadership.
