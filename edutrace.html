Engineered for Uncompromising Focus

Every feature in EduTrace is built to eliminate digital friction and build repeatable academic discipline.

High-Precision Focus Stopwatch

Tracks net productive study intervals, break durations, and distraction events down to the second. Backed by StudyTimerService and SessionManager so your session survives background memory trims, lock screens, and reboot events.

Academic Hierarchy (Degrees & Subjects)

Structure multiple concurrent learning programs — such as BBA in Accounting, Chartered Accountancy (ICAB), or Professional Certifications. Configure custom syllabus goals, target hourly milestones, and color themes.

Library Mode & Distraction Shield

Automatically activates Android Do Not Disturb and silences ringer interruptions during active focus intervals. The App Usage Warden detects when distracting social applications are launched, displaying overlay nudges to curb mindless scrolling.

Study Pulse & 24h Routine Analytics

Gain objective intelligence on your daily circadian cycle. Compute focus ratios, visualize lost time vs. deep work intervals across 24-hour day charts, and inspect home screen statistics with the bundled StudyPulseWidget.

In-App GitHub Auto-Updates & OBB

Zero-friction updates: EduTrace queries GitHub Releases to notify you of new versions, downloading packages via Android's native DownloadManager. The OTA OBB Asset Pack system updates study quotes and ambient soundscapes on the fly.

Optional Cloud Backup & Leaderboards

Connect seamlessly to the optional EduTrace REST API server with encrypted JWT authentication to backup your records and participate in student focus rankings without compromising private notes.
