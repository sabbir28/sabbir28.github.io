Operational Convergence: Discipline Meets Technology

How statutory financial rules and computational tools integrate into a reliable, repeatable production workflow.

Phase 01 // Verification

Double-Entry Ledger Integrity

Structuring chart of accounts compliant with IFRS and IAS 1. Enforcing strict debits-equal-credits parity across journal entries, trial balance aggregation, and subledger reconciliation before any analytical operations begin.

Phase 02 // Extraction & Cleansing

Computational Data Wrangling

Deploying scripted Python (Pandas) routines and relational MySQL queries to cleanse raw transactional datasets, remove inconsistencies, identify outlier anomalies, and normalize financial periods for stress-testing.

Phase 03 // Valuation & Analytics

Dynamic Financial Modeling

Executing 3-stage DuPont decomposition (ROE = Net Profit Margin × Total Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier), horizontal and vertical statement normalization, cash conversion cycle mapping, and sensitivity analysis in Microsoft Excel.

Phase 04 // Governance & Decision Support

Audit-Ready Executive Reporting

Translating complex multi-period financial tables into high-conviction executive summaries, variance dashboards, and board-level disclosures equipped with complete audit verification trails.
